## 8. Product Core

Target loop: personal meaning -> one daily action -> short reset -> avatar/identity feedback -> visible progression -> next-day hook.

MVP testable claim: users should understand and complete the full daily loop in under two minutes, then report that the avatar/progress cue makes the action feel more personally meaningful.

## 9. Risk Register

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fragmentation | high | Force one daily flow; avoid separate feature tabs as MVP core. |
| Avatar novelty churn | high | Tie avatar changes to completed actions and progress, not one-off image generation. |
| Astrology trust/overclaiming | high | Use soft framing, confidence labels, and avoid deterministic claims. |
| AI hallucination in guidance | high | Constrain advice to low-stakes coaching prompts and clear disclaimers. |
| Subscription fatigue | medium | Keep daily loop free; monetize depth/personal analysis/advanced progress. |
| Crowded adjacent markets | medium | Position around integrated transformation loop, not generic astrology/mindfulness/avatar. |

## 10. Go/No-Go Status

Current status: conditional go for continued validation, not yet go for full product build.

Go signals present:

- Adjacent markets have meaningful revenue pools.
- Users are already trained on daily loops, streaks, reflection, and spiritual/personalized guidance.
- Top-100 evidence suggests avatar/identity is common but behavior-tied avatar progression is rare.

Remaining proof required:

- Manual validation of the top-100 candidates.
- Reviews/forums for user pain language and subscription objections.
- Pricing/IAP extraction beyond App Store metadata.
- Prototype test of the two-minute daily loop.
