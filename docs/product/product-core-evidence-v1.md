## Product Implication

Alina should not rely on any single pillar as the moat. The moat candidate is the sequence and the causal link: completing the daily action changes the identity/avatar feedback object.

## MVP Testable Claim

Users should understand and complete the full daily loop in under two minutes, then report that the avatar/progress cue makes the action feel more personally meaningful.
