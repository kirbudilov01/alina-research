## Scope

This file summarizes public forum/source signals discovered for Alina's five market pillars. It is a source map and qualitative evidence layer, not a statistically representative user study.

Primary data file: `data_raw/forum_evidence_signals.csv`

## Cross-Market Patterns

- Accuracy and trust matter most when the product touches astrology, belief, identity, or life guidance.
- Users like daily anchors, but they dislike systems that become punitive, manipulative, or noisy.
- Habit and productivity discussions repeatedly recommend fewer tracked items and compact routine blocks.
- Streaks are a double-edged retention tool: they can create early motivation, but strict reset-to-zero mechanics create anxiety and churn.
- Mindfulness users show sensitivity to paywalls, notifications, streaks, and gamification when those mechanics interrupt calm.
- Companion/avatar demand exists, but emotional support products need bounded claims, safety posture, and clear role framing.
- Gaming/progression sources support repeatable loops and visible rewards, but forum signals warn against daily-claim monetization vibes.

## Implications For Alina

1. The core loop should be one small daily ritual, not a broad tracker.
2. Progression should show identity/effort becoming visible, but with forgiveness and recovery mechanics.
3. The reset/mindfulness layer should feel calm and low-pressure; game mechanics should not invade the moment of regulation.
4. Astrology/spiritual guidance must be explainable, soft-framed, and non-deterministic.
5. The paywall should sit behind demonstrated daily value, with deeper personalization/content as paid layers.
6. Missed days should trigger reflection or gentle recovery, not shame or total progress loss.
