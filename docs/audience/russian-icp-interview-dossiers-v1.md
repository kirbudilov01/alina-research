# Русские ICP interview dossiers V1

Собрано: 2026-05-31T12:55:43.533Z

## Зачем нужен этот слой

Этот dossier переводит H5 из audience matrix в исполнимую программу интервью. По каждому ICP видно: кого искать, через какие каналы, какие шесть тестов провести, какие evidence fields заполнить, что усилит сегмент и что его ослабит. Это не заменяет интервью; это делает их сопоставимыми и защищает отчет от выбора аудитории по вкусу.

Всего ICP dossiers: 6. P0 segments: ICP_A, ICP_D. Capture rows: 96. Completed rows: 0. Пока completed rows равны нулю, H5 остается directionally supported, но не validated.

## ICP_A. Spiritual self-improvers

**Core job:** Turn symbolic/personal meaning into one grounded action today.

**Entry behavior:** Uses astrology, tarot, manifestation, devotional, journaling, or guidance apps to make today feel meaningful.

**Recruiting:** community_or_accountability_need: Relevant community thread, Discord, forum, or group where users already discuss the job | word_of_mouth_or_personal_recommendation: Warm referral or friend-of-user intro | coded_forum_need_or_competitor_context: Forum-language recruiting using exact problem wording from coded snippets | social_platform_discovery: Social platform discovery or creator/community mention

**Tests:** ICP_A_T01: screener -> recent_behavior_match=yes/no | ICP_A_T02: problem_interview -> specific_episode + workaround + pain_intensity_1_5 | ICP_A_T03: prototype_loop -> comprehension=yes/no; meaning_lift_1_5; loop_completion | ICP_A_T04: positioning_test -> preferred_concept; differentiation_1_5 | ICP_A_T05: willingness_to_pay -> current_paid_behavior=yes/no; acceptable_price_range; paid_feature_rank | ICP_A_T06: disconfirmation -> fatal_objection=yes/no; top_objection

**Prompt pack:** screener: Ask: "Which apps, rituals, games, journals, coaches, avatars, or guidance tools did you use in the last 30 days, and what triggered the last use?" Match against: Uses astrology, tarot, manifestation, devotional, journaling, or guidance apps to make today feel meaningful. | problem_interview: Ask for the last real moment when they needed this job: Turn symbolic/personal meaning into one grounded action today.. Capture exact language, workaround, emotional stakes, and what they tried instead. | willingness_to_pay: Ask what they currently pay for in astrology_esoterics|coaching, then test paid depth: richer analysis, custom rituals, advanced avatar/progress history, and coaching-style review. | disconfirmation: Ask directly: "What would make this feel unsafe, cringe, manipulative, generic, or not for you?" Probe against known risk: Alina needs careful framing: soft guidance, no deterministic claims, visible limits, and clear safety posture.

**Capture:** recent_behavior_match|specific_episode|current_tool|verbatim_language|loop_comprehension|meaning_lift_1_5|differentiation_1_5|return_intent_1_5|paid_depth_feature|fatal_objection

**Upgrade:** апгрейдить primary ICP, если recent behavior, trust, meaning lift и WTP не противоречат spiritual/self-improvement framing.

**Downgrade:** ослабить сегмент, если участники не называют recent behavior, проблема оказывается абстрактной, paid depth отвергается, или возникает fatal objection: Alina needs careful framing: soft guidance, no deterministic claims, visible limits, and clear safety posture.

**Ethics:** Post transparently, follow community rules, and offer a short paid or clearly voluntary research session.
