## Highest-Signal Interpretation

- The largest cluster is not generic wellness demand; it is desire for more depth, options, customization, and accessibility after users understand the basic product.
- The strongest Alina-specific opportunity remains visible progress: users like effort becoming visible through streaks, levels, characters, rewards, and growth metaphors.
- The sharpest execution risk is reliability. When a ritual app breaks, the product does not merely lose utility; it breaks emotional trust.
- Subscription complaints are common enough that monetization should sit behind a proven daily loop, not before it.
- Trust/accuracy and spiritual safety concerns are smaller by row count but strategically important because Alina touches guidance, belief, identity, and emotion.
