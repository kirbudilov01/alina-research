## Product Implications

- Lightweight browser tools validate that habit capture, progress display, accountability, and AI feedback can live in very small surfaces.
- The strongest adjacent examples still appear to stop at utility loops: habit, stat, reminder, blocker, or narrow AI suggestion.
- This supports a sharper Alina claim: the product should not compete as a generic habit tracker or AI coach; it should make a completed daily action visibly change a personal identity/avatar feedback object.
- The main validation risk is hidden mechanics: screenshots or onboarding may reveal richer identity metaphors than metadata exposes.
